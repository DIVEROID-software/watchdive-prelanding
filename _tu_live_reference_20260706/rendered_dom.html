FAQ

Good questions, short answers.

Is Watch Dive a standalone dive computer?+

No. Watch Dive turns your compatible smartwatch into a dive computer solution using the housing, sensor, and connected app.

How deep can I use it?+

Watch Dive is rated to 60 m, with a recommended recreational operating depth of 40 m.

Does it support safety stop and no-decompression limits?+

Yes. It supports key recreational dive functions including safety stop, ascent-rate alert, depth, dive time, temperature, and no-decompression guidance.

Does it work for freediving?+

Yes. Watch Dive supports both scuba diving and freediving.
