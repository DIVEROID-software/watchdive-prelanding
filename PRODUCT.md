# WatchDive landing context

## Confirmed scope

WatchDive is a smartwatch housing and app for scuba diving and freediving. A compatible smartwatch is sold separately. This repository is the pre-launch landing and signup flow; the primary outcome is genuine email signups followed by email verification. Verification is not purchase intent or a sale.

Visitors arrive mainly from mobile advertising across multiple markets. The shared landing serves ten locales and two diving segments. Preserve the existing approved compatibility, depth, price, subscription and product claims in the localized copy.

## Owner's November 5 direction

The large early signup panel feels premature. Build a preview with spacious photography and white space, inspired by the supplied Prelaunch campaign examples. Replace the purple brutalist panels, fluorescent borders and offset shadows. Let visitors understand the product before asking for their email. Keep a direct signup link for visitors already ready to join.

The new variant disables automatic entry and exit signup popups. Existing popup components and their measurement remain active in the unchanged control. The owner's latest direction is to compare both public destinations using Malaysia ads at KRW50,000/day per variant, after stopping previous directly operated ads.

## Content and conversion constraints

- Use the official WatchDive logo and existing approved product photos, eight approved beta-test quotes and eight sourced press logos. Do not invent endorsement, ratings or demand.
- Preserve the $149 first-100-Kickstarter-backers terms and signup-does-not-reserve distinction in the price section and beside any signup benefit that mentions $149. Email signup is free, with no payment required.
- Preserve locale routing, raw campaign queries, signup source IDs, consent, verification, OAuth, optional phone, reservation and attribution behavior.
- The current request authorizes the public landing comparison and its two Meta budgets. Root alone operates live advertising. The initial 72-hour pilot is a bounded operator choice within the stated daily budgets; it is not a separate owner duration reply. No unrelated CRM, customer email or payment changes follow from it.

## Evaluation

Judge the new variant on product clarity, readable mobile layout, discoverable signup, accessible controls and intact existing conversion contracts. It puts the first form immediately after product usage and features, keeps the two proof sections together after it, makes the launch alert the hero's primary action, and uses existing localized benefit/qualification/verification copy. Compare simultaneous cohorts through exact campaign/adset/ad IDs and original layout exposure. Report new signups, verified signups within 48 hours of signup, acquisition cost and attribution gaps separately. Until a Meta randomized split is actually registered and verified, describe the two adsets as a concurrent observational comparison, not causal proof.
